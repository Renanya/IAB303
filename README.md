# IAB303

## Assignment 2 TOWS Analysis
The objective of this assignment was to undertake the role of a data analyst in a small QLD business supplying luxury car hires. The aim is to look into intiiatives to scale back petrol vehicles in the company's fleet in favour of environmentally friendly options such as hybrids and EVs. By utilising 3 datasets which included: the company's fleet of vehicles, the QLD EV charging network, and Guardian OpenPlatform. This assignment tested a mix of spatial, structured and unstructured data which was then used for visulations in aiding my decision making and reasoning in a TOWS analysis. 

For the assignment, the objectives were to:

    1. A clear description of the business concern and key stakeholders
    2. At least one analysis of structured data with appropriate visualisation
    3. At least one analysis of semi-structured data with appropriate visualisation
    4. Detailed markdown descriptions of your thinking for each analysis as well as addressing relevant human factors and potential ethical concerns
    5. A detailed TOWS analysis for at least one combination of structured and semi-structured data, including appropriate recommended actions that address the business concern. These recommendations should also address relevant human factors and potential ethical concerns.
    6. Peer feedback notes including: (a) The names and student numbers of the members of your group; (b) Feedback that you received and how you addressed it; and (c) Feedback that you provided to others in your group.
    7. A presentation of your recommendations
